## Product description

Crosslify is an AI tool for individual ecommerce sellers. Users upload a product photo and generate localized product marketing images, titles, descriptions, keywords, and platform-ready assets for marketplaces such as Amazon, TikTok Shop, Etsy, eBay, Shopee, Lazada, Temu, and AliExpress.

## Target customers

Individual ecommerce sellers, online shop owners, creators, and small marketplace sellers in the United States and other international markets.

## Pricing

- Free: $0/mo, 5 images per month
- Starter: $9/mo, 50 images per month
- Growth: $19/mo, 200 images per month
- Pro: $49/mo, 500 images per month

## Refund policy summary

First subscriptions are eligible for a refund within 7 days if usage is below 5 generated images. Customers can request a refund by emailing support@crosslify.com with their account email, plan name, purchase date, and reason.
